1. Getting Started

Logging In

  1. Open the Production Tracker in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Click Sign In.

If you don't have login credentials, contact your administrator.

Using the Help Button

Every page has a gold "?" button in the bottom-right corner. Click it to open a help overlay with step-by-step instructions for that specific page. You can close it by clicking the X, clicking outside the box, or pressing Esc.

3. Work Orders

Searching & Filtering

  1. Use the search bar to find orders by WO number, customer name, or part number.
  2. Filter by Status (In Progress, Completed, On Hold).
  3. Filter by Stage to see orders at a specific production step.
  4. Filter by Part Number to see only orders for a specific windshield.
  5. Click Clear to reset all filters.

Click any row in the table to open that work order's detail page.

Creating a New Work Order

  1. Click the New Work Order button.
  2. Fill in the required fields (marked with *):
    • WO Number — must be unique; this is what gets printed on the barcode
    • Customer Name
  3. Select a Part Number from the dropdown. If the part you need isn't listed, an admin must add it in Settings first.
  4. Fill in optional fields as needed: Serial Number, Contract, Aircraft info, Notes, etc.
  5. Click Create Work Order.

The order starts at Stage 1 automatically and you'll be taken to its detail page.

Importing Work Orders (Admin Only)

  1. From the Work Orders page, click Import.
  2. Click Download Template to get the Excel file with correct column headers.
  3. Fill in your data. Required: WO Number. Mark each stage column Y (perform) or N (skip). Delete the instructions row.
  4. Click Choose File and select your filled-in spreadsheet.
  5. Review the preview — valid rows show green, errors show red.
  6. Click Import All Valid to create the work orders.
Note: Stage 1 and Stage 15 are always required and cannot be skipped. All imported orders start at Stage 1.

4. Work Order Detail

This is the main workspace for a single work order — where you advance stages, manage documents, view issued parts, and track history.

Advancing to the Next Stage

  1. Click the Advance Stage button.
  2. If this stage has BOM parts assigned, review the parts that will be deducted from inventory.
  3. Optionally add notes about the work performed.
  4. Click Confirm.

The system automatically deducts BOM parts from inventory and records the transition in the stage history.

Inspection (Stage 3) — Special

When advancing through Stage 3 (Inspection), you'll be presented with a checklist of all stages. Select which stages should be skipped for this particular order. The system will automatically jump over skipped stages during subsequent advances.

Why skip stages? Not every windshield needs all 15 production steps. The inspector evaluates the windshield and determines which stages are necessary for this specific repair.

Placing On Hold & Resuming

To place on hold:

  1. Click the Hold button.
  2. Enter the reason for the hold (required).
  3. Click Confirm Hold.

The order shows a red "ON HOLD" banner and cannot advance until resumed.

To resume:

Click the Resume button. The order returns to normal status and can be advanced again.

Documents & Photos

  1. Click Upload or drag-and-drop files into the drop zone.
  2. Accepted formats: PDF, PNG, JPG, GIF, WEBP, and HEIC (iPhone photos).
  3. iPhone photos (HEIC format) are automatically converted to JPG for compatibility.
  4. Hover over a document to view (eye icon) or delete (trash icon) it.
File size limit: 10 MB per file.

Editing & Reverting

Editing order details:

Click the pencil icon next to "Order Details" to edit fields. Click Save Changes when done.

Warning: Changing the part number will reverse all previously issued BOM parts and re-issue the correct parts for the new BOM.

Reverting to the previous stage:

Click Go Back (when visible) to revert the order one stage. Any parts issued when entering the current stage will be restored to inventory.

5. Scan & Advance

The Scan page is designed for tablets and mobile devices on the shop floor. Use it to quickly look up and advance work orders.

Using a Barcode Scanner

  1. Navigate to the Scan page (or tap the Scan icon in the nav).
  2. Place your cursor in the scan input field.
  3. Scan the barcode on the work order label — the WO number fills in automatically.
  4. The order's current stage and status appear.
  5. Click Advance to move to the next stage.

Manual Entry

  1. Type the WO number in the input field.
  2. Click Lookup or press Enter.
  3. Review the order information, then click Advance.
Tip: After advancing, the input clears so you can scan the next order immediately. If the stage has BOM parts, you'll confirm them before advancing.

6. Adding a New Windshield (Complete Workflow)

This is the most common source of confusion. Adding a new windshield part number requires three steps across three pages, done in order:

The 3-Step Workflow

1. Settings
Add part number
➔
2. Inventory
Add subcomponents
➔
3. BOM
Link parts to stages

Step 1: Add the Part Number (Settings Page)

  1. Go to the Settings page.
  2. In the "Windshield Part Numbers" section, click Add.
  3. Enter the part number and an optional description.
  4. Click Save.

The new part number is now available in the work order dropdown.

Step 2: Add Subcomponents (Inventory Page)

Check if the new windshield needs any subcomponents (materials/parts) that don't already exist in inventory.

  1. Go to the Inventory page.
  2. Review existing subcomponents — if the parts you need are already there, skip to Step 3.
  3. For each new subcomponent, click Add Part.
  4. Enter the part number, description, category, unit of measure, initial quantity, reorder point, and other details.
  5. Click Save.
Remember: Subcomponents are shared across all windshields. If a material is already in inventory (e.g., sealant, bolts), you don't need to add it again — you'll reuse it in the BOM.

Step 3: Define the BOM (BOM Page)

  1. Go to the BOM page.
  2. Select the new windshield from the Part Number dropdown.
  3. Click Add Item for each subcomponent needed.
  4. For each item:
    • Select the subcomponent from the dropdown
    • Choose which stage it should be issued at
    • Enter the quantity required
    • Add optional notes
  5. Click Save.
  6. Repeat for every subcomponent needed at every stage.
Done! The windshield is now fully configured. When work orders for this part number advance through each stage, the correct subcomponents will be automatically deducted from inventory.

8. Inventory Management

The Inventory page manages subcomponents — the individual parts and materials used in windshield production. These are the items that get assigned to BOMs and automatically deducted during production.

Adding a New Subcomponent

  1. Click Add Part.
  2. Enter: part number, description, category, unit of measure, quantity on hand.
  3. Set a reorder point to trigger low-stock alerts on the dashboard.
  4. Optionally add: unit cost, reorder quantity, lead time, and supplier.
  5. Click Save.

After adding a subcomponent here, it becomes available in the BOM page for assignment to windshield part numbers.

Receiving Stock

  1. Click Receive Stock.
  2. Select the part from the dropdown.
  3. Enter the quantity received.
  4. Click Receive — the quantity on hand updates immediately.

9. Bill of Materials (BOM)

The BOM defines which subcomponents are needed for each windshield part number, and at which production stage they should be issued. Admin access required.

How It Works

Each windshield has its own BOM. When a work order advances to a stage, the system checks the BOM for that windshield and stage, then automatically deducts the required parts from inventory.

Viewing a BOM

  1. Select a windshield part number from the dropdown at the top.
  2. The BOM displays organized by production stage, showing each subcomponent, quantity, and notes.

Adding a BOM Item

  1. Click Add Item.
  2. Select the subcomponent from the dropdown.
  3. Choose the production stage it should be issued at.
  4. Enter the quantity required.
  5. Add optional notes.
  6. Click Save.
Subcomponent not in the dropdown? Go to the Inventory page and add it first. The dropdown shows all parts from Inventory.

Editing & Removing Items

  • Click the pencil icon to change a line item's stage, quantity, or notes.
  • Click the trash icon to remove a line item.
  • Changes affect future stage advances — already-issued parts are not affected.
Tip: A subcomponent can appear at multiple stages. For example, if sealant is applied at both Stage 5 and Stage 12, add two separate BOM entries.

12. Tips & Troubleshooting

Best Practices

  • Scan, don't type — use barcode scanning whenever possible to reduce errors.
  • Keep inventory updated — receive stock promptly to maintain accurate availability.
  • Use holds appropriately — always document the reason clearly for team communication.
  • Check Low Stock daily — review dashboard alerts to prevent production delays.
  • Add notes when advancing — document any issues or observations at each stage.
  • Use the Help button — every page has a "?" button with context-specific instructions.

Common Issues & Solutions

ProblemSolution
"Database connection error"Check your network connection and refresh the page. If it persists, contact your administrator.
"Part number not found"The part number needs to be added in Settings first (admin required).
"Insufficient inventory"Check the Inventory page for current stock levels. Receive new stock or adjust the BOM if quantities are incorrect.
Label printing issuesEnsure the correct printer is selected and paper size matches your label roll (DK-1208 recommended).
Order stuck on holdClick Resume on the order detail page. Check the hold reason if unclear why it was held.
New feature not appearingDo a hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R
iPhone photo won't uploadHEIC photos are supported and auto-converted to JPG. If conversion fails, convert the photo to JPG on your phone first.
Can't find subcomponent in BOM dropdownAdd the subcomponent in the Inventory page first, then return to BOM.
